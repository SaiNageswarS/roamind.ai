Give three separate judgements, each 0-10.

1. novelty -- is there a genuinely new discovery or result here?
   8-10 a first report of a new finding
   5-7  incremental progress or a fresh angle on recent work
   0-4  a restatement, an explainer or old news

2. significance -- does it widen our understanding of the world?
   8-10 changes how scientists see a question
   5-7  a solid contribution to its field
   0-4  marginal

3. credibility -- is it sound?
   8-10 peer-reviewed or from a reputable research institution, accurately reported
   5-7  a preprint or a careful report
   0-4  sensational, speculative or unsourced

include (true/false) -- set false for opinion pieces, obituaries, policy and funding news
with no science in it, event announcements and job posts.
