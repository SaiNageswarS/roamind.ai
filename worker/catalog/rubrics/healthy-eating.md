Give three separate judgements, each 0-10.

1. evidence -- is it based on sound nutrition research?
   8-10 a well-designed study, a review, or guidance from a reputable body
   5-7  reasonable, with some evidence behind it
   0-4  a fad, a single small study oversold, or no evidence

2. practicality -- does it translate into simple, wholesome eating choices?
   8-10 a clear change the reader can make
   5-7  background worth knowing
   0-4  nothing actionable

3. positivity -- does it encourage healthy habits rather than fear of food?
   8-10 encouraging and balanced
   5-7  neutral
   0-4  alarmist, shaming or restrictive

include (true/false) -- set false for supplement or product marketing, diet fads,
weight-loss promises and items unrelated to food and nutrition.
