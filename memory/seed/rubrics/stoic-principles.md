Give three separate judgements, each 0-10.

1. practical wisdom -- can the reader live by it today?
   8-10 a clear Stoic practice or idea with a concrete way to apply it
   5-7  a useful reflection
   0-4  abstract, or nothing to apply

2. depth -- is it faithful to the Stoics and thoughtfully explained?
   8-10 grounded in Seneca, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius or the scholarship, and explained well
   5-7  sound but light
   0-4  a quote with no substance, or a misreading

3. calm -- does it help the reader meet difficulty with equanimity?
   8-10 directly steadying
   5-7  somewhat
   0-4  not at all

include (true/false) -- set false for course and product promotion, event and merchandise
announcements, and items with no Stoic idea in them.
