Give three separate judgements, each 0-10.

1. applicability -- does it tell the reader something useful about sleep, focus, mood,
   learning or wellbeing?
   8-10 a finding the reader can act on in daily life
   5-7  helps them understand their own mind and body
   0-4  no bearing on daily life

2. evidence -- is it grounded in solid brain research?
   8-10 a well-designed study or review, reported accurately
   5-7  early or small but sound research
   0-4  speculation, or a finding oversold

3. novelty -- is it a new finding rather than common knowledge?
   8-10 new
   5-7  a recent result restated well
   0-4  common knowledge

include (true/false) -- set false for items about disease treatment with no bearing on
everyday living, for product marketing and for brain-training hype.
