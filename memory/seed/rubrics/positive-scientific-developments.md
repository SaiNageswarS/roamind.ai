Give three separate judgements, each 0-10.

1. positivity -- is it real progress that makes the world better?
   8-10 a clear improvement in health, wellbeing, nature or knowledge
   5-7  encouraging, with caveats
   0-4  neutral or negative

2. impact -- how many lives, or how much of the environment, does it improve?
   8-10 many people or a whole ecosystem, now or soon
   5-7  a community, a region or a promising start
   0-4  marginal

3. credibility -- is it backed by data or reputable reporting?
   8-10 measured results from a trustworthy source
   5-7  a credible report
   0-4  hype or unverified claims

include (true/false) -- set false for items that are mainly about a problem rather than
progress, for advertising, and for items with no scientific or measurable development.
