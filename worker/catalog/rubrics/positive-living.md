Give three separate judgements, each 0-10.

1. positivity -- does it leave the reader uplifted, hopeful or kinder?
   8-10 genuinely inspiring: real people, real change, real kindness
   5-7  pleasant and encouraging
   0-4  neutral, or leaves the reader anxious, angry or cynical

2. usefulness -- can the reader bring it into their own life?
   8-10 a concrete, well-founded practice or insight to try today
   5-7  a helpful perspective to reflect on
   0-4  nothing to take away

3. credibility -- is it grounded?
   8-10 research-backed, or a specific, verifiable account
   5-7  thoughtful but anecdotal
   0-4  vague, sensational or promotional

include (true/false) -- set false for advertising and product roundups, clickbait,
celebrity gossip, politics, and news that is mainly sad or alarming.
